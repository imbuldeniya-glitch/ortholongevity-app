Common questions

What is your Knee Age? Your questions, answered.

What is my knee age?

Your knee age is the biological age of your knees, which can be younger or older than the age on your passport. The Knee Age™ assessment scores you across five clinical domains in around 60 seconds and gives you a single number comparing your knees' biological age to your chronological age.

How old are my knees?

Probably not the same age as you. Cartilage health, muscle strength, movement quality, injury history and underlying biology all push knee age up or down. The free Knee Age™ test gives you an evidence-informed estimate in about a minute.

Is this the first knee age calculator?

Yes. Knee Age™ is the first surgeon-built knee-specific biological age assessment, launched in 2025 and created by Dr Arj Imbuldeniya, a London consultant orthopaedic knee and hip surgeon. Heart Age changed how people think about their hearts; Knee Age™ does the same for your joints.

What about my other joints, how old are my joints overall?

Knee Age™ focuses on the knee, the joint most likely to limit how you move and age. Many of the domains it measures, such as muscle strength, movement quality and metabolic health, affect every joint in your body, so your result is a strong signal for your wider joint health too.

Is the Knee Age test a medical diagnosis?

No. It is an educational assessment, not a diagnosis, and it does not replace a consultation with a doctor. If you have knee pain, swelling, instability or an injury, seek a proper clinical assessment.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. The Knee Age™ test is free and takes around 60 seconds.

How do I make my knees younger?

The biggest levers are usually building strength around the joint, steadier metabolic health, and better daily movement. Your genetics and past injuries set the starting line, not the finish. The free Knee Age test shows which single change would take the most years off your Knee Age, so you start where it counts.

What is a good knee score?

A good result is a Knee Age at or below your real age, with your five domains sitting on the healthy side. It means your knees are ageing more slowly than you are. The test shows exactly which domains are helping and which are holding you back.

Can you guess my knee age?

It is an honest estimate, not a scan. But the five domains it measures, strength, movement, injury history, metabolic health and genetic risk, are the ones that genuinely drive how knees age, so the number is a useful and motivating starting point. If anything in your answers needs a closer look, the test will tell you to get it assessed.

Knee health, made practical

How to make your knees younger

Your knees age fastest when the muscle around them weakens, your metabolic health drifts, and daily movement drops. Rebuild those and the trajectory changes. Progressive strength two or three times a week, steadier blood sugar and better sleep, and simply breaking up long hours of sitting are the levers that move knee health the most, and they compound quietly over years.

Genetics and old injuries set your starting line, not your finish. That is the heart of knee longevity: acting early and consistently on the things you can change, so your joints stay capable for decades rather than declining on schedule. The fastest way to know where to start is to measure it. Take the free Knee Age test and see which single change would take the most years off your knees.
